# Starlight 8 Module 1 — Breaking News

Designed for 1:1 revision (approximately 60 minutes; final simulation can be assigned for independent study).

## Syllabus and limitations
Based on Starlight 8 Module 1 topics corroborated by publicly available teacher-book and course-plan references. This is an original practice resource, not an official publisher test. The teacher's exact assessment may differ.

## Lesson route
1. News context (5 min)
2. Disaster vocabulary and collocations (10 min)
3. Present/Past tense contrasts (12 min)
4. Quantifiers, future, relative pronouns (8 min)
5. Word formation (7 min)
6. Phrasal verbs, lexical contrasts, participles (7 min)
7. Reading and writing (6 min + homework)
8. Mock test (5 min + homework)

## Tutor prompts
After each checkpoint ask: 'What clue helped you choose?', 'Why are the other options wrong?', and 'Can you make your own example?'

## Additional coverage to check against teacher's materials
Official Revision 1 and Test 1 may include infinitive/gerund, comparative forms, keyword transformations, dialogues, complaints and more extended writing/listening. The site focuses on the most common Module 1 themes and formats but is not a guarantee of complete alignment with a particular school's test.

## Marking
Multiple-choice tasks auto-check. Writing is deliberately not auto-marked; assess task achievement, organisation, vocabulary, grammar and accuracy with the student. The short model is practice guidance, not a mandated exam response.


## Real Exam Test 1
Original scans supplied by the teacher are embedded unaltered as images. The response sheets below are separate interactive controls. A–E have the original supplied answer key; F is not included because the listening page/audio were not supplied. G writing is tutor assessed (200–250 words). The teacher key scan is concealed by default. Exam mode includes a 50-minute countdown, but only the objective questions are automatically scored. Double-click, click, tap or keyboard activation opens the A2+ vocabulary dictionary for selected words in the interactive HTML text; the source scans remain untouched.


## F — Reading adaptation of original Listening
The supplied original Listening F and Marcus Shaver transcript are embedded as unchanged scans. Students read the interview and complete the original ten gaps. This measures reading, not listening. Key: 1 eight days; 2 snowstorm; 3 world; 4 MP3 player; 5 shelter; 6 ridge; 7 moving; 8 feet; 9 companion; 10 awake. The interview scan also includes the beginning of Test 2; this is original source material, not part of the assigned task.
